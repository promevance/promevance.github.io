Hello, I'm Evan

I'm a brand and growth specialist and GTM engineer with 8+ years of experience growing startups and business ventures across Africa and Europe. I've worked with more than 75 businesses in nine industries, from health-tech and fintech to real estate, logistics and FMCG, sharpening their positioning, building growth systems and executing with discipline.

My main site, evanpromise.com, tells that story and holds the brand and growth case studies. This site is its technical companion.

Where data fits in

Most of the growth work I'm proud of came down to the same steps: frame the problem, find the audience that matters, decide what to say to them using data, then execute. I'm now building out that data side properly as a budding data engineer, across three areas:

  • Business intelligence: dashboards and data stories in Tableau and Power BI.
  • Data analysis: cleaning, modelling and testing data in Python, SQL and Excel.
  • Data science: machine learning such as customer segmentation with scikit-learn.

On the GTM engineering side, I build automated outbound and lifecycle workflows with Clay, n8n and CRM tooling, connecting product, marketing and sales data.

How to read this portfolio

  • Short on time? Open any project and read the "At a glance" box at the top. It gives the question, the finding and the recommendation in about a minute.
  • Want the detail? The "Under the hood" section covers data, method, definitions, code and limitations, and links to the notebooks, data and Tableau files.
  • Want to check my work? Everything is on GitHub, including a script that re-derives the Spotify headline numbers independently of Tableau.
